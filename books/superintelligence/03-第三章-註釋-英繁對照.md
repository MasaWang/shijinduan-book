<a id="note-3"></a>

## 註 3

**English**

At least a millionfold speedup compared to human brains is physically possible, as can been seen by considering the difference in speed and energy of relevant brain processes in comparison to more efficient information processing. The speed of light is more than a million times greater than that of neural transmission, synaptic spikes dissipate more than a million times more heat than is thermodynamically necessary, and current transistor frequencies are more than a million times faster than neuron spiking frequencies (Yudkowsky [2008a]; see also Drexler [1992]). The ultimate limits of speed superintelligence are bounded by light-speed communications delays, quantum limits on the speed of state transitions, and the volume needed to contain the mind (Lloyd 2000). The “ultimate laptop” described by Lloyd (2000) would run a 1.4×10²¹ FLOPS brain emulation at speedup of 3.8×10²⁹× (assuming the emulation could be sufficiently parallelized). Lloyd’s construction, however, is not intended to be technologically plausible; it is only meant to illustrate those constraints on computation that are readily derivable from basic physical laws.

**繁體中文**

相較於人類大腦，至少一百萬倍的加速，在物理上是可能的。只要比較相關大腦過程與更有效率的資訊處理，在速度和能量上的差異，就能看出這一點。光速超過神經傳導速度的一百萬倍；突觸尖峰活動所耗散的熱量，超過熱力學上必要熱量的一百萬倍；而目前電晶體的頻率，也超過神經元尖峰發放頻率的一百萬倍（Yudkowsky [2008a]；另見 Drexler [1992]）。速度超級智慧的最終極限，受到光速通訊延遲、狀態轉換速度的量子極限，以及容納該心智所需體積的約束（Lloyd 2000）。Lloyd（2000）描述的「終極筆記型電腦」，能以 3.8×10²⁹ 倍的加速率，運行一個需要 1.4×10²¹ FLOPS 的大腦模擬體，前提是該模擬能充分平行化。不過，Lloyd 提出的構造並不是要在技術上具有可行性，而只是用來說明那些可以直接從基本物理定律推導出的運算限制。

## 註 13

**English**

<a id="note-14"></a>

## 註 14

**English**

By the Church–Turing thesis, all computable functions are computable by a Turing machine. Since any of the three forms of superintelligence could simulate a Turing machine (if given access to unlimited memory and allowed to operate indefinitely), they are by this formal criterion computationally equivalent. Indeed, an average human being (provided with unlimited scrap paper and unlimited time) could also implement a Turing machine, and thus is also equivalent by the same criterion. What matters for our purposes, however, is what these different systems can achieve in practice, with finite memory and in reasonable time. And the efficiency variations are so great that one can readily make some distinctions. For example, a typical individual with an IQ of 85 could be taught to implement a Turing machine. (Conceivably, it might even be possible to train some particularly gifted and docile chimpanzee to do this.) Yet, for all practical intents and purposes, such an individual is presumably incapable of, say, independently developing general relativity theory or of winning a Fields medal.

**繁體中文**

依據邱奇–圖靈論題（Church–Turing thesis），所有可計算函數，都能由圖靈機（Turing machine）計算。三種超級智慧中的任何一種，只要能使用無限記憶體，並獲准無限期運作，就能模擬圖靈機；因此，按照這個形式標準，它們在計算能力上是等價的。事實上，只要提供無限的草稿紙與無限時間，一個普通人也能實作圖靈機，所以依照同一標準，也同樣等價。然而，就我們的目的而言，重要的是：這些不同系統在記憶有限、時間合理的條件下，實際上能做到什麼。它們的效率差異如此巨大，很容易就能作出一些區分。例如，可以教會一位典型的、智商為 85 的人實作圖靈機。（可以設想，甚至也許能訓練某隻特別有天分且溫順的黑猩猩做到這件事。）然而，就所有實際目的而言，這樣的人大概無法獨立發展出廣義相對論，或獲得菲爾茲獎。

**English**

Note that this survey of sources of machine advantage is disjunctive: our argument succeeds even if some of the items listed are illusory, so long as there is at least one source that can provide a sufficiently large advantage.

**繁體中文**

請注意，這份對機器優勢來源的考察，採取的是析取式（disjunctive）論證：只要至少有一項來源能帶來足夠大的優勢，即使列出的某些項目其實並不存在，我們的論證仍然成立。
