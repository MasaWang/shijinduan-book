# Chapter 3｜第三章

# Forms of superintelligence
# 超級智慧的形式

Nick Bostrom 著｜英繁對照翻譯初稿

> 編排說明：英文在前、繁體中文在後；跨頁段落與轉檔斷行已接回，科學記號依 PDF 恢復。數值、技術現況與推測保留 2014 年原著的歷史時點。

**English**

So what, exactly, do we mean by “superintelligence”? While we do not wish to get bogged down in terminological swamps, something needs to be said to clarify the conceptual ground. This chapter identifies three different forms of superintelligence, and argues that they are, in a practically relevant sense, equivalent. We also show that the potential for intelligence in a machine substrate is vastly greater than in a biological substrate. Machines have a number of fundamental advantages which will give them overwhelming superiority. Biological humans, even if enhanced, will be outclassed.

**繁體中文**

那麼，我們所說的「超級智慧」（superintelligence），究竟是什麼意思？我們雖不想陷入術語的泥淖，仍有必要釐清概念的基礎。本章區分三種不同形式的超級智慧，並主張：就具有實際意義的某個層面而言，它們是等價的。我們也將說明，機器基質所能承載的智慧，其潛力遠大於生物基質。機器具有若干根本優勢，將使它們取得壓倒性的優勢。生物人類即使經過增強，也將望塵莫及。

---

**English**

Many machines and nonhuman animals already perform at superhuman levels in narrow domains. Bats interpret sonar signals better than man, calculators outperform us in arithmetic, and chess programs beat us in chess. The range of specific tasks that can be better performed by software will continue to expand. But although specialized information processing systems will have many uses, there are additional profound issues that arise only with the prospect of machine intellects that have enough general intelligence to substitute for humans across the board.

**繁體中文**

許多機器和非人類動物，早已在狹窄領域中表現出超越人類的能力。蝙蝠比人類更善於解讀聲納訊號，計算機的算術能力勝過我們，西洋棋程式也能在棋盤上擊敗我們。軟體能夠做得更好的特定任務，其範圍將持續擴大。然而，專門化的資訊處理系統雖然用途廣泛，另有一些深刻的問題，只有當機器智慧體有望具備足夠的通用智慧（general intelligence）、能在各方面取代人類時，才會浮現。

---

**English**

As previously indicated, we use the term “superintelligence” to refer to intellects that greatly outperform the best current human minds across many very general cognitive domains. This is still quite vague. Different kinds of system with rather disparate performance attributes could qualify as superintelligences under this definition. To advance the analysis, it is helpful to disaggregate this simple notion of superintelligence by distinguishing different bundles of intellectual super-capabilities. There are many ways in which such decomposition could be done. Here we will differentiate between three forms: speed superintelligence, collective superintelligence, and quality superintelligence.

**繁體中文**

如前所述，我們以「超級智慧」指稱這樣的智慧體：在許多相當廣泛的認知領域中，其表現都遠遠超過當今最優秀的人類心智。這仍然相當模糊。依照這一定義，性能特徵頗為不同的各類系統，都可能算是超級智慧。為了推進分析，有必要把這個簡單的超級智慧概念拆開，區分幾組不同的超常智力。這種拆分可以有許多方式。這裡，我們將區分三種形式：速度超級智慧（speed superintelligence）、集體超級智慧（collective superintelligence），以及品質超級智慧（quality superintelligence）。

## Speed superintelligence
## 速度超級智慧

**繁體中文**

速度超級智慧是一種與人類心智相同、但運作更快的智慧體。就概念而言，這是最容易分析的超級智慧形式。〔[註 1](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-1)〕我們可以將速度超級智慧定義如下：

---

**English**

Speed superintelligence: A system that can do all that a human intellect can do, but much faster.

**繁體中文**

速度超級智慧：能做到人類智慧體所能做的一切，而且快得多的系統。

---

**English**

By “much” we here mean something like “multiple orders of magnitude.” But rather than try to expunge every remnant of vagueness from the definition, we will entrust the reader with interpreting it sensibly.〔[註 2](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-2)〕

**繁體中文**

這裡的「多」，大致是指「好幾個數量級」。不過，我們不打算把定義中每一絲模糊之處都清除殆盡，而是交由讀者作合理理解。〔[註 2](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-2)〕

---

**English**

The simplest example of speed superintelligence would be a whole brain emulation running on fast hardware.〔[註 3](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-3)〕 An emulation operating at a speed of ten thousand times that of a biological brain would be able to read a book in a few seconds and write a PhD thesis in an afternoon. With a speedup factor of a million, an emulation could accomplish an entire millennium of intellectual work in one working day.〔[註 4](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-4)〕

**繁體中文**

速度超級智慧最簡單的例子，就是在高速硬體上運行的全腦模擬（whole brain emulation）。〔[註 3](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-3)〕若一個模擬體的運行速度是生物大腦的一萬倍，它就能在幾秒內讀完一本書，並在一個下午寫出一篇博士論文。若加速至一百萬倍，模擬體就能在一個工作日內，完成整整一千年的智力工作。〔[註 4](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-4)〕

---

**English**

To such a fast mind, events in the external world appear to unfold in slow motion. Suppose your mind ran at 10,000×. If your fleshly friend should happen to drop his teacup, you could watch the porcelain slowly descend toward the carpet over the course of several hours, like a comet silently gliding through space toward an assignation with a far-off planet; and, as the anticipation of the coming crash tardily propagates through the folds of your friend’s gray matter and from thence out into his peripheral nervous system, you could observe his body gradually assuming the aspect of a frozen oops—enough time for you not only to order a replacement cup but also to read a couple of scientific papers and take a nap.

**繁體中文**

對這樣高速運作的心智而言，外界的事件彷彿都以慢動作展開。假設你的心智以一萬倍的速度運作。要是你那有血有肉的朋友不小心掉落了茶杯，你便可以在好幾個小時裡，看著瓷杯緩緩降向地毯，猶如一顆彗星無聲地掠過太空，赴向與遙遠行星的約會；而當即將摔碎茶杯的預感，慢吞吞地穿過朋友灰質的皺褶，再由那裡傳向周邊神經系統時，你可以看著他的身體逐漸擺出一副凝固的「糟了」模樣——這段時間不只足夠你訂購一個替代的杯子，還夠你讀上幾篇科學論文，再小睡一會兒。

---

**English**

Because of this apparent time dilation of the material world, a speed superintelligence would prefer to work with digital objects. It could live in virtual reality and deal in the information economy. Alternatively, it could interact with the physical environment by means of nanoscale manipulators, since limbs at such small scales could operate faster than macroscopic appendages. (The characteristic frequency of a system tends to be inversely proportional to its length scale.〔[註 5](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-5)〕) A fast mind might commune mainly with other fast minds rather than with bradytelic, molasses-like humans.

**繁體中文**

由於物質世界呈現出這種時間彷彿被拉長的現象，速度超級智慧會偏好處理數位物件。它可以生活在虛擬實境中，參與資訊經濟。另一種選擇是透過奈米尺度的操縱器與實體環境互動，因為如此微小的肢體能比巨觀尺度的附肢運作得更快。（系統的特徵頻率往往與其長度尺度成反比。〔[註 5](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-5)〕）高速心智可能主要與其他高速心智交流，而非與遲緩得像糖蜜般的人類往來。

---

**English**

The speed of light becomes an increasingly important constraint as minds get faster, since faster minds face greater opportunity costs in the use of their time for traveling or communicating over long distances.〔[註 6](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-6)〕 Light is roughly a million times faster than a jet plane, so it would take a digital agent with a mental speedup of 1,000,000× about the same amount of subjective time to travel across the globe as it does a contemporary human journeyer. Dialing somebody long distance would take as long as getting there “in person,” though it would be cheaper as a call would require less bandwidth. Agents with large mental speedups who want to converse extensively might find it advantageous to move near one another. Extremely fast minds with need for frequent interaction (such as members of a work team) may take up residence in computers located in the same building to avoid frustrating latencies.

**繁體中文**

心智運作得越快，光速就越成為重要的限制，因為高速心智若把時間用於長途旅行或通訊，便得承擔更大的機會成本。〔[註 6](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-6)〕光的速度大約是噴射機的一百萬倍，因此，對心智加速至一百萬倍的數位行動者（digital agent）而言，跨越地球所花的主觀時間，大致相當於當代人類旅客的旅程。打長途電話所需的時間，會和「親自」前往一樣長，只是通話所需的頻寬較少，因此成本較低。心智大幅加速、又想頻繁交談的行動者，可能會發現搬到彼此附近更有利。需要經常互動的極高速心智，例如同一工作團隊的成員，或許會入住同一棟建築裡的電腦，以免通訊延遲令人不耐。

---

## Collective superintelligence
## 集體超級智慧

**English**

Another form of superintelligence is a system achieving superior performance by aggregating large numbers of smaller intelligences:

**繁體中文**

另一種形式的超級智慧，是透過匯集大量較小的智慧體，取得卓越表現的系統：

---

**English**

Collective superintelligence: A system composed of a large number of smaller intellects such that the system’s overall performance across many very general domains vastly outstrips that of any current cognitive system.

**繁體中文**

集體超級智慧：由大量較小智慧體構成的系統，其在許多相當廣泛領域中的整體表現，遠遠超越目前任何認知系統。

---

**English**

Collective superintelligence is less conceptually clear-cut than speed superintelligence.〔[註 7](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-7)〕 However, it is more familiar empirically. While we have no experience with human-level minds that differ significantly in clock speed, we do have ample experience with collective intelligence, systems composed of various numbers of human-level components working together with various degrees of efficiency. Firms, work teams, gossip networks, advocacy groups, academic communities, countries, even humankind as a whole, can—if we adopt a somewhat abstract perspective—be viewed as loosely defined “systems” capable of solving classes of intellectual problems. From experience, we have some sense of how easily different tasks succumb to the efforts of organizations of various size and composition.

**繁體中文**

集體超級智慧在概念上不如速度超級智慧那樣清晰。〔[註 7](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-7)〕不過，就經驗而言，我們對它更為熟悉。我們沒有見識過運作時脈差異極大、卻同樣具有人類水準的心智，卻對集體智慧有豐富的經驗：這些系統由數量不等、具有人類水準的組成單位，以不同的效率協力運作。只要採取稍微抽象的觀點，企業、工作團隊、閒談網絡、倡議團體、學術社群、國家，乃至整個人類，都可以被視為邊界較為鬆散、能解決某些類型智力問題的「系統」。從經驗中，我們多少知道，不同規模與組成的組織，面對不同任務時，解決起來有多容易。

---

**English**

Collective intelligence excels at solving problems that can be readily broken into parts such that solutions to sub-problems can be pursued in parallel and verified independently. Tasks like building a space shuttle or operating a hamburger franchise offer myriad opportunities for division of labor: different engineers work on different components of the spacecraft; different staffs operate different restaurants. In academia, the rigid division of researchers, students, journals, grants, and prizes into separate self-contained disciplines—though unconducive to the type of work represented by this book—might (only in a conciliatory and mellow frame of mind) be viewed as a necessary accommodation to the practicalities of allowing large numbers of diversely motivated individuals and teams to contribute to the growth of human knowledge while working relatively independently, each plowing their own furrow.

**繁體中文**

集體智慧擅長解決容易拆分的問題：各個子問題的解答可以平行尋求，也能獨立驗證。建造太空梭或經營漢堡連鎖店之類的任務，提供了無數分工機會：不同工程師處理太空船的不同零件，不同員工經營不同餐廳。在學術界，研究人員、學生、期刊、研究經費與獎項，都被嚴格劃分到各自封閉的學科中。這雖然不利於本書這類工作，但也許——只有在心平氣和、願意退讓時——可以把它視為對現實需要的必要遷就：讓動機各異的大量個人與團隊，能在各耕其田、相對獨立工作的同時，為人類知識的增長作出貢獻。

---

**English**

A system’s collective intelligence could be enhanced by expanding the number or the quality of its constituent intellects, or by improving the quality of their organization.〔[註 8](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-8)〕 To obtain a collective superintelligence from any present-day collective intelligence would require a very great degree of enhancement. The resulting system would need to be capable of vastly outperforming any current collective intelligence or other cognitive system across many very general domains. A new conference format that lets scholars exchange information more effectively, or a new collaborative information-filtering algorithm that better predicted users’ ratings of books and movies, would clearly not on its own amount to anything approaching collective superintelligence. Nor would a 50% increase in the world population, or an improvement in pedagogical method that enabled students to complete a school day in four hours instead of six. Some far more extreme growth of humanity’s collective cognitive capacity would be required to meet the criterion of collective superintelligence.

**繁體中文**

增加組成智慧體的數量、提高其品質，或改善其組織方式，都能增強系統的集體智慧。〔[註 8](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-8)〕要把任何現有的集體智慧提升為集體超級智慧，需要極大程度的增強。產生的系統必須能在許多相當廣泛的領域中，遠遠勝過目前任何集體智慧或其他認知系統。一種讓學者更有效交換資訊的新型會議形式，或一套更能預測使用者對書籍和電影評分的新協作式資訊篩選演算法，顯然都不會單憑自身就接近集體超級智慧。世界人口增加百分之五十，或改良教學方法，讓學生以四小時完成原本六小時的學校課程，也同樣不夠。人類的集體認知能力必須出現極端得多的增長，才能符合集體超級智慧的標準。

---

**English**

Note that the threshold for collective superintelligence is indexed to the performance levels of the present—that is, the early twenty-first century. Over the course of human prehistory, and again over the course of human history, humanity’s collective intelligence has grown by very large factors. World population, for example, has increased by at least a factor of a thousand since the Pleistocene.〔[註 9](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-9)〕 On this basis alone, current levels of human collective intelligence could be regarded as approaching superintelligence relative to a Pleistocene baseline. Some improvements in communications technologies—especially spoken language, but perhaps also cities, writing, and printing—could also be argued to have, individually or in combination, provided super-sized boosts, in the sense that if another innovation of comparable impact to our collective intellectual problem-solving capacity were to happen, it would result in collective superintelligence.〔[註 10](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-10)〕

**繁體中文**

請注意，集體超級智慧的門檻，是以目前——也就是二十一世紀初——的表現水準為基準。無論在人類的史前時代，還是在有文字記載的歷史中，人類的集體智慧都曾大幅倍增。例如，自更新世以來，世界人口至少增加了一千倍。〔[註 9](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-9)〕光憑這一點，若以更新世為基準，當今人類的集體智慧就可以被認為已接近超級智慧。也可以主張，某些通訊技術的進步——尤其是口語，但或許也包括城市、文字與印刷——單獨或共同帶來了超大幅度的提升。這裡的意思是：如果又有一項創新，對我們集體運用智力解決問題的能力產生同等影響，那麼它就會造就集體超級智慧。〔[註 10](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-10)〕

---

**English**

A certain kind of reader will be tempted at this point to interject that modern society does not seem so particularly intelligent. Perhaps some unwelcome political decision has just been made in the reader’s home country, and the apparent unwisdom of that decision now looms large in the reader’s mind as evidence of the mental incapacity of the modern era. And is it not the case that contemporary humanity is idolizing material consumption, depleting natural resources, polluting the environment, decimating species diversity, all the while failing to remedy screaming global injustices and neglecting paramount humanistic or spiritual values? However, setting aside the question of how modernity’s shortcomings stack up against the not-so-inconsiderable failings of earlier epochs, nothing in our definition of collective superintelligence implies that a society with greater collective intelligence is necessarily better off. The definition does not even imply that the more collectively intelligent society is wiser. We can think of wisdom as the ability to get the important things approximately right. It is then possible to imagine an organization composed of a very large cadre of very efficiently coordinated knowledge workers, who collectively can solve intellectual problems across many very general domains. This organization, let us suppose, can operate most kinds of businesses, invent most kinds of technologies, and optimize most kinds of processes. Even so, it might get a few key big-picture issues entirely wrong— for instance, it may fail to take proper precautions against existential risks—and as a result pursue a short explosive growth spurt that ends ingloriously in total collapse. Such an organization could have a very high degree of collective intelligence; if sufficiently high, the organization is a collective superintelligence. We should resist the temptation to roll every normatively desirable attribute into one giant amorphous concept of mental functioning, as though one could never find one admirable trait without all the others being equally present. Instead, we should recognize that there can exist instrumentally powerful information processing systems—intelligent systems—that are neither inherently good nor reliably wise. But we will revisit this issue in Chapter 7.

**繁體中文**

有一類讀者，此時可能忍不住插話，說現代社會似乎並沒有特別聰明。也許讀者的祖國剛作出某個不受歡迎的政治決定，而這項決定顯而易見的不明智，如今便在讀者心中成了現代人心智能力不足的突出證據。再說，當代人類難道不是一邊崇拜物質消費、耗竭自然資源、污染環境、摧殘物種多樣性，一邊未能糾正全球極其嚴重的不公，又忽視至關重要的人文或精神價值嗎？然而，先不談現代的缺失與過往時代那些同樣不可小覷的弊病相比如何，我們對集體超級智慧的定義，完全不意味著集體智慧更高的社會必然處境更好。這個定義甚至不意味著集體智慧更高的社會會更明智。我們可以把明智（wisdom）理解為一種能力：在重要的事情上，大致作出正確判斷。如此一來，我們就可以設想一個組織：它由數量龐大、協調極其有效率的知識工作者構成，能集體解決許多相當廣泛領域中的智力問題。假定這個組織能經營大多數類型的企業、發明大多數類型的技術，並最佳化大多數類型的流程。即便如此，它仍可能在少數攸關全局的關鍵問題上大錯特錯——例如，未能對存續性風險（existential risks）採取適當的預防措施——結果只經歷一段短暫的爆發式增長，便以全面崩潰告終，黯然收場。這樣的組織可能具有極高程度的集體智慧；如果高到一定程度，它就是集體超級智慧。我們應當抵抗一種誘惑：把所有在價值上值得追求的特質，揉成一個龐大而模糊的心智功能概念，彷彿只要找到一項值得讚賞的特質，其餘特質就必然同樣具備。我們應該承認：可能存在一些在達成目的方面極其強大的資訊處理系統——有智慧的系統——它們既非本質良善，也不總能明智行事。不過，我們會在第七章再談這個問題。

---

**English**

Collective superintelligence could be either loosely or tightly integrated. To illustrate a case of loosely integrated collective superintelligence, imagine a planet, MegaEarth, which has the same level of communication and coordination technologies that we currently have on the real Earth but with a population one million times as large. With such a huge population, the total intellectual workforce on MegaEarth would be correspondingly larger than on our planet. Suppose that a scientific genius of the caliber of a Newton or an Einstein arises at least once for every 10 billion people: then on MegaEarth there would be 700,000 such geniuses living contemporaneously, alongside proportionally vast multitudes of slightly lesser talents. New ideas and technologies would be developed at a furious pace, and global civilization on MegaEarth would constitute a loosely integrated collective superintelligence.〔[註 11](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-11)〕

**繁體中文**

集體超級智慧的整合程度，可以鬆散，也可以緊密。為了說明鬆散整合的情況，請想像一顆名為「巨型地球」（MegaEarth）的行星：它的通訊與協調技術，和我們現實地球目前的水準相同，人口卻是一百萬倍。人口如此龐大，巨型地球的智力勞動者總數，也會相應地多於我們的星球。假設每一百億人中，至少會出現一位牛頓或愛因斯坦等級的科學天才，那麼，巨型地球上便會有七十萬位這樣的天才同時在世，還有數量按比例同樣龐大、才華稍遜一籌的人群。新觀念與新技術將以驚人的速度湧現，巨型地球的全球文明便會構成一個鬆散整合的集體超級智慧。〔[註 11](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-11)〕

---

**English**

If we gradually increase the level of integration of a collective intelligence, it may eventually become a unified intellect—a single large “mind” as opposed to a mere assemblage of loosely interacting smaller human minds.〔[註 12](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-12)〕 The inhabitants of MegaEarth could take steps in that direction by improving communications and coordination technologies and by developing better ways for many individuals to work on any hard intellectual problem together. A collective superintelligence could thus, after gaining sufficiently in integration, become a “quality superintelligence.”

**繁體中文**

如果我們逐步提高集體智慧的整合程度，它最終可能成為一個統一的智慧體——一個巨大的「心智」，而不只是許多較小人類心智鬆散互動的集合。〔[註 12](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-12)〕巨型地球的居民可以改善通訊與協調技術，並發展更好的方法，讓許多人共同處理任何艱難的智力問題，藉此朝那個方向前進。因此，集體超級智慧在整合程度提高到一定程度後，可能成為「品質超級智慧」。

## Quality superintelligence
## 品質超級智慧

**English**

We can distinguish a third form of superintelligence.

**繁體中文**

我們可以區分出第三種形式的超級智慧。

---

**English**

Quality superintelligence: A system that is at least as fast as a human mind and vastly qualitatively smarter.

**繁體中文**

品質超級智慧：運作速度至少與人類心智相同，而且在質的層面上聰明得多的系統。

---

**English**

As with collective intelligence, intelligence quality is also a somewhat murky concept; and in this case the difficulty is compounded by our lack of experience with any variations in intelligence quality above the upper end of the present human distribution. We can, however, get some grasp of the notion by considering some related cases.

**繁體中文**

和集體智慧一樣，智慧品質（intelligence quality）也是一個有些模糊的概念；在這裡，困難還更大，因為我們沒有見識過任何超出當今人類智慧品質分布上端的變化。不過，考慮幾個相關的情況，仍能讓我們對這個概念有所掌握。

---

**English**

First, we can expand the range of our reference points by considering nonhuman animals, which have intelligence of lower quality. (This is not meant as a speciesist remark. A zebrafish has a quality of intelligence that is excellently adapted to its ecological needs; but the relevant perspective here is a more anthropocentric one: our concern is with performance on humanly relevant complex cognitive tasks.) Nonhuman animals lack complex structured language; they are capable of no or only rudimentary tool use and tool construction; they are severely restricted in their ability to make long-term plans; and they have very limited abstract reasoning ability. Nor are these limitations fully explained by a lack of speed or of collective intelligence among nonhuman animal minds. In terms of raw computational power, human brains are probably inferior to those of some large animals, including elephants and whales. And although humanity’s complex technological civilization would be impossible without our massive advantage in collective intelligence, not all distinctly human cognitive capabilities depend on collective intelligence. Many are highly developed even in small, isolated hunter–gatherer bands.〔[註 13](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-13)〕 And many are not nearly as highly developed among highly organized nonhuman animals, such as chimpanzees and dolphins intensely trained by human instructors, or ants living in their own large and well-ordered societies. Evidently, the remarkable intellectual achievements of Homo sapiens are to a significant extent attributable to specific features of our brain architecture, features that depend on a unique genetic endowment not shared by other animals. This observation can help us illustrate the concept of quality superintelligence: it is intelligence of quality at least as superior to that of human intelligence as the quality of human intelligence is superior to that of elephants’, dolphins’, or chimpanzees’.

**繁體中文**

首先，我們可以把智慧品質較低的非人類動物納入考慮，藉此擴大參照範圍。（這並非出於物種歧視。斑馬魚的智慧品質極其適合牠的生態需求；不過，此處採取的是較以人類為中心的觀點：我們關心的是，在對人類有意義的複雜認知任務上，表現如何。）非人類動物缺乏結構複雜的語言；牠們不會使用或製造工具，或只能做到十分初步的程度；牠們制定長期計畫的能力受到嚴重限制，抽象推理能力也極其有限。這些限制，也不能全然歸因於非人類動物心智的速度不足或集體智慧不足。若單看原始運算能力，人類大腦很可能不如某些大型動物，包括大象與鯨。雖然沒有我們在集體智慧上的巨大優勢，人類複雜的技術文明便不可能存在，但並非所有人類特有的認知能力，都仰賴集體智慧。許多能力即使在小型、孤立的狩獵採集群體中，也已高度發展。〔[註 13](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-13)〕而在組織程度很高的非人類動物中，許多能力的發展卻遠遠不及人類，例如接受人類教練密集訓練的黑猩猩與海豚，或生活在龐大而井然有序社會中的螞蟻。顯然，智人卓越的智力成就，在相當大程度上源自我們大腦架構的某些特定特徵，而這些特徵又依賴其他動物所不具備的獨特遺傳稟賦。這項觀察可以幫助我們說明品質超級智慧的概念：它在智慧品質上超越人類的程度，至少就像人類智慧品質超越大象、海豚或黑猩猩的程度一樣。

---

**English**

A second way to illustrate the concept of quality superintelligence is by noting the domain-specific cognitive deficits that can afflict individual humans, particularly deficits that are not caused by general dementia or other conditions associated with wholesale destruction of the brain’s neurocomputational resources. Consider, for example, individuals with autism spectrum disorders who may have striking deficits in social cognition while functioning well in other cognitive domains; or individuals with congenital amusia, who are unable to hum or recognize simple tunes yet perform normally in most other respects. Many other examples could be adduced from the neuropsychiatric literature, which is replete with case studies of patients suffering narrowly circumscribed deficits caused by genetic abnormalities or brain trauma. Such examples show that normal human adults have a range of remarkable cognitive talents that are not simply a function of possessing a sufficient amount of general neural processing power or even a sufficient amount of general intelligence: specialized neural circuitry is also needed. This observation suggests the idea of possible but non-realized cognitive talents, talents that no actual human possesses even though other intelligent systems— ones with no more computing power than the human brain—that did have those talents would gain enormously in their ability to accomplish a wide range of strategically relevant tasks.

**繁體中文**

說明品質超級智慧概念的第二種方式，是留意個別人類可能出現的特定領域認知缺損，尤其是那些並非由整體性的失智，或其他會大規模破壞大腦神經運算資源的病況所造成的缺損。例如，有些自閉症類群障礙者，在社會認知上可能有顯著缺損，在其他認知領域卻運作良好；又如先天性失歌症（congenital amusia）患者，無法哼唱或辨認簡單旋律，但在其他大多數方面表現正常。神經精神醫學文獻中還能找出許多例子：其中充滿個案研究，描述患者因遺傳異常或腦部創傷，而出現範圍極為局限的缺損。這些例子顯示，正常成年人擁有一系列出色的認知才能，並不僅僅因為具備足夠的一般神經處理能力，甚至也不僅僅因為具備足夠的通用智慧：專門化的神經迴路也不可或缺。這項觀察引出一個想法：可能存在一些尚未實現的認知才能，沒有任何現存人類擁有它們，但其他智慧系統——即使運算能力不超過人類大腦——若具備這些才能，就能大幅提高完成各種具有策略意義之任務的能力。

---

**English**

Accordingly, by considering nonhuman animals and human individuals with domain-specific cognitive deficits, we can form some notion of different qualities of intelligence and the practical difference they make. Had Homo sapiens lacked (for instance) the cognitive modules that enable complex linguistic representations, it might have been just another simian species living in harmony with nature. Conversely, were we to gain some new set of modules giving an advantage comparable to that of being able to form complex linguistic representations, we would become superintelligent.

**繁體中文**

因此，透過考察非人類動物，以及具有特定領域認知缺損的人類個體，我們可以對不同的智慧品質，以及它們造成的實際差異，形成一些理解。假如智人缺少了例如讓複雜語言表徵成為可能的認知模組，我們或許只會是另一種與自然和諧共存的猿猴物種。反過來說，如果我們獲得一組新模組，帶來的優勢足以媲美形成複雜語言表徵的能力，我們就會成為超級智慧。

---

## Direct and indirect reach
## 直接與間接能力範圍

**English**

Superintelligence in any of these forms could, over time, develop the technology necessary to create any of the others. The indirect reaches of these three forms of superintelligence are therefore equal. In that sense, the indirect reach of current human intelligence is also in the same equivalence class, under the supposition that we are able eventually to create some form of superintelligence. Yet there is a sense in which the three forms of superintelligence are much closer to one another: any one of them could create other forms of superintelligence more rapidly than we can create any form of superintelligence from our present starting point.

**繁體中文**

上述任何一種形式的超級智慧，經過一段時間，都能發展出創造其他形式所需的技術。因此，這三種形式的超級智慧，其間接能力範圍（indirect reach）是相同的。從這個意義上說，假定我們最終能創造出某種形式的超級智慧，那麼，當今人類智慧的間接能力範圍，也屬於同一個等價類。不過，在另一個意義上，這三種超級智慧彼此更為接近：它們之中的任何一種，都能比我們從目前起點創造任何一種超級智慧，更快地創造出其他形式的超級智慧。

---

**English**

The direct reaches of the three different forms of superintelligence are harder to compare. There may be no definite ordering. Their respective capabilities depend on the degree to which they instantiate their respective advantages— how fast a speed superintelligence is, how qualitatively superior a quality superintelligence is, and so forth. At most, we might say that, ceteris paribus, speed superintelligence excels at tasks requiring the rapid execution of a long series of steps that must be performed sequentially while collective superintelligence excels at tasks admitting of analytic decomposition into parallelizable sub-tasks and tasks demanding the combination of many different perspectives and skill sets. In some vague sense, quality superintelligence would be the most capable form of all, inasmuch as it could grasp and solve problems that are, for all practical purposes, beyond the direct reach of speed superintelligence and collective superintelligence.〔[註 14](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-14)〕

**繁體中文**

三種超級智慧的直接能力範圍（direct reach）則比較難以比較，也許根本不存在明確的排序。它們各自的能力，取決於各自優勢實現到什麼程度——速度超級智慧究竟有多快、品質超級智慧在質的層面上究竟優越多少，等等。我們最多也許只能說，在其他條件相同的情況下，速度超級智慧擅長處理必須迅速執行一長串依序步驟的任務；集體超級智慧則擅長處理能經由分析拆分為可平行執行之子任務的工作，以及需要結合多種觀點與技能的工作。從某種模糊的意義上說，品質超級智慧會是其中能力最強的一種，因為它能理解並解決某些問題，而就一切實際用途而言，這些問題都超出了速度超級智慧與集體超級智慧的直接能力範圍。〔[註 14](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-14)〕

---

**English**

In some domains, quantity is a poor substitute for quality. One solitary genius working out of a cork-lined bedroom can write In Search of Lost Time. Could an equivalent masterpiece be produced by recruiting an office building full of literary hacks?〔[註 15](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-15)〕 Even within the range of present human variation we see that some functions benefit greatly from the labor of one brilliant mastermind as opposed to the joint efforts of myriad mediocrities. If we widen our purview to include superintelligent minds, we must countenance a likelihood of there being intellectual problems solvable only by superintelligence and intractable to any ever-so-large collective of non-augmented humans.

**繁體中文**

在某些領域，數量很難代替品質。一位獨自待在軟木襯壁臥室裡工作的天才，就能寫出《追憶似水年華》（In Search of Lost Time）。招募整整一棟辦公大樓的平庸文人，能寫出同等的傑作嗎？〔[註 15](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-15)〕即使只看當今人類的差異範圍，我們也能發現，有些工作交由一位才智卓絕的人操持，比起無數平庸之輩的共同努力，會有大得多的效益。如果把眼光擴展到超級智慧心智，我們就必須正視這樣一種可能性：有些智力問題只有超級智慧能夠解決，無論由多少未經增強的人類組成集體，都無法處理。

---

**English**

There might thus be some problems that are solvable by a quality superintelligence, and perhaps by a speed superintelligence, yet which a loosely integrated collective superintelligence cannot solve (other than by first amplifying its own intelligence).〔[註 16](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-16)〕 We cannot clearly see what all these problems are, but we can characterize them in general terms.〔[註 17](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-17)〕 They would tend to be problems involving multiple complex interdependencies that do not permit of independently verifiable solution steps: problems that therefore cannot be solved in a piecemeal fashion, and that might require qualitatively new kinds of understanding or new representational frameworks that are too deep or too complicated for the current edition of mortals to discover or use effectively. Some types of artistic creation and strategic cognition might fall into this category. Some types of scientific breakthrough, perhaps, likewise. And one can speculate that the tardiness and wobbliness of humanity’s progress on many of the “eternal problems” of philosophy are due to the unsuitability of the human cortex for philosophical work. On this view, our most celebrated philosophers are like dogs walking on their hind legs—just barely attaining the threshold level of performance required for engaging in the activity at all.〔[註 18](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-18)〕

**繁體中文**

因此，可能有些問題，品質超級智慧能夠解決，速度超級智慧也許也能解決，鬆散整合的集體超級智慧卻無法解決，除非先增強自己的智慧。〔[註 16](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-16)〕我們無法清楚看出這些問題究竟包括哪些，但可以概略描述它們的特性。〔[註 17](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-17)〕它們往往涉及多重而複雜的相互依賴關係，求解步驟無法各自獨立驗證，因此不能零敲碎打地解決；它們也許需要在質的層面上全新的理解方式，或新的表徵框架，而這些框架過於深奧或複雜，當前版本的凡人無法發現，也無法有效使用。某些藝術創作與策略認知，可能屬於這一類。某些科學突破，也許同樣如此。我們還可以推測，人類在哲學的許多「永恆問題」上，之所以進展遲緩、搖擺不定，是因為人類的大腦皮質不適合從事哲學工作。依照這個看法，我們最負盛名的哲學家，就像用後腿走路的狗——才勉強跨過了能從事這項活動的最低表現門檻。〔[註 18](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-18)〕

## Sources of advantage for digital intelligence
## 數位智慧的優勢來源

**English**

Minor changes in brain volume and wiring can have major consequences, as we see when we compare the intellectual and technological achievements of humans with those of other apes. The far greater changes in computing resources and architecture that machine intelligence will enable will probably have consequences that are even more profound. It is difficult, perhaps impossible, for us to form an intuitive sense of the aptitudes of a superintelligence; but we can at least get an inkling of the space of possibilities by looking at some of the advantages open to digital minds. The hardware advantages are easiest to appreciate:

**繁體中文**

大腦體積與連線方式的微小變化，就可能帶來重大後果；比較人類與其他類人猿的智力和技術成就，就能看出這一點。機器智慧將使運算資源與架構出現大得多的變化，其後果很可能也更加深遠。我們很難、甚至可能無法，對超級智慧的才能形成直觀理解；但透過考察數位心智可能享有的一些優勢，至少能略窺其可能性的範圍。硬體上的優勢最容易理解：

---

**English**

• Speed of computational elements. Biological neurons operate at a peak speed of about 200 Hz, a full seven orders of magnitude slower than a modern microprocessor (~ 2 GHz).〔[註 19](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-19)〕 As a consequence, the human brain is forced to rely on massive parallelization and is incapable of rapidly performing any computation that requires a large number of sequential operations.〔[註 20](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-20)〕 (Anything the brain does in under a second cannot use much more than a hundred sequential operations—perhaps only a few dozen.) Yet many of the most practically important algorithms in programming and computer science are not easily parallelizable. Many cognitive tasks could be performed far more efficiently if the brain’s native support for parallelizable pattern-matching algorithms were complemented by, and integrated with, support for fast sequential processing.

**繁體中文**

• 運算元件的速度。生物神經元的最高運作速度約為 200 Hz，比現代微處理器（約 2 GHz）整整慢了七個數量級。〔[註 19](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-19)〕因此，人類大腦不得不仰賴大規模平行處理，無法迅速執行需要大量循序操作的運算。〔[註 20](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-20)〕（大腦能在一秒內完成的任何事情，都不可能用上遠超過一百次的循序操作——也許只有幾十次。）然而，程式設計與電腦科學中，許多在實務上最重要的演算法，都不容易平行化。假如大腦原本對可平行化之模式比對演算法的支援，能夠加上對高速循序處理的支援，並將兩者整合，許多認知任務就能更有效率地完成。

---

**English**

• Internal communication speed. Axons carry action potentials at speeds of 120 m/s or less, whereas electronic processing cores can communicate optically at the speed of light (300,000,000 m/s).〔[註 21](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-21)〕 The sluggishness of neural signals limits how big a biological brain can be while functioning as a single processing unit. For example, to achieve a round-trip latency of less than 10 ms between any two elements in a system, biological brains must be smaller than 0.11 m³. An electronic system, on the other hand, could be 6.1×10¹⁷ m³, about the size of a dwarf planet: eighteen orders of magnitude larger.〔[註 22](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-22)〕

**繁體中文**

• 內部通訊速度。軸突傳遞動作電位（action potentials）的速度不超過每秒 120 公尺，而電子處理核心能以光學方式，按光速（每秒 300,000,000 公尺）通訊。〔[註 21](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-21)〕神經訊號的遲緩，限制了生物大腦在仍能作為單一處理單元運作的前提下，究竟能有多大。例如，要讓系統內任意兩個元件之間的來回延遲低於 10 毫秒，生物大腦的體積就必須小於 0.11 立方公尺。電子系統則可以達到 6.1×10¹⁷ 立方公尺，約為一顆矮行星的大小：足足大了十八個數量級。〔[註 22](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-22)〕

---

**English**

• Number of computational elements. The human brain has somewhat fewer than 100 billion neurons.〔[註 23](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-23)〕 Humans have about three and a half times the brain size of chimpanzees (though only one-fifth the brain size of sperm whales).〔[註 24](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-24)〕 The number of neurons in a biological creature is most obviously limited by cranial volume and metabolic constraints, but other factors may also be significant for larger brains (such as cooling, development time, and signal-conductance delays—see the previous point). By contrast, computer hardware is indefinitely scalable up to very high physical limits.〔[註 25](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-25)〕 Supercomputers can be warehouse-sized or larger, with additional remote capacity added via high-speed cables.〔[註 26](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-26)〕

**繁體中文**

• 運算元件的數量。人類大腦的神經元數量略少於一千億。〔[註 23](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-23)〕人類大腦的大小約為黑猩猩的三倍半，不過只有抹香鯨的五分之一。〔[註 24](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-24)〕生物體的神經元數量，最明顯受到顱腔體積與代謝條件的限制；但對更大的大腦而言，其他因素也可能相當重要，例如散熱、發育時間與訊號傳導延遲，見前一點。相較之下，在極高的物理上限以內，電腦硬體可以持續擴展。〔[註 25](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-25)〕超級電腦可以有倉庫那麼大，甚至更大，還能透過高速纜線加入額外的遠端運算資源。〔[註 26](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-26)〕

---

**English**

• Storage capacity. Human working memory is able to hold no more than some four or five chunks of information at any given time.〔[註 27](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-27)〕 While it would be misleading to compare the size of human working memory directly with the amount of RAM in a digital computer, it is clear that the hardware advantages of digital intelligences will make it possible for them to have larger working memories. This might enable such minds to intuitively grasp complex relationships that humans can only fumblingly handle via plodding calculation.〔[註 28](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-28)〕 Human long-term memory is also limited, though it is unclear whether we manage to exhaust its storage capacity during the course of an ordinary lifetime—the rate at which we accumulate information is so slow. (On one estimate, the adult human brain stores about one billion bits—a couple of orders of magnitude less than a low-end smartphone.〔[註 29](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-29)〕) Both the amount of information stored and the speed with which it can be accessed could thus be vastly greater in a machine brain than in a biological brain.

**繁體中文**

• 儲存容量。人類的工作記憶（working memory），在任一時刻最多只能維持大約四、五個資訊組塊（chunks）。〔[註 27](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-27)〕直接把人類工作記憶的大小，與數位電腦的隨機存取記憶體（RAM）容量相比，會產生誤導；但數位智慧的硬體優勢，顯然會讓它們可能擁有更大的工作記憶。這或許能使這類心智直觀地掌握複雜關係，而人類只能靠緩慢的計算，笨拙地處理這些關係。〔[註 28](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-28)〕人類的長期記憶同樣有限，不過，我們在一般壽命內，是否真會用盡其儲存容量，仍不清楚——我們累積資訊的速度實在太慢。（依一項估計，成年人的大腦儲存約十億位元，比低階智慧型手機少了幾個數量級。〔[註 29](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-29)〕）因此，無論儲存的資訊量，還是存取資訊的速度，機器大腦都有可能遠遠超過生物大腦。

---

**English**

• Reliability, lifespan, sensors, etc. Machine intelligences might have various other hardware advantages. For example, biological neurons are less reliable than transistors.〔[註 30](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-30)〕 Since noisy computing necessitates redundant encoding schemes that use multiple elements to encode a single bit of information, a digital brain might derive some efficiency gains from the use of reliable high-precision computing elements. Brains become fatigued after a few hours of work and start to permanently decay after a few decades of subjective time; microprocessors are not subject to these limitations. Data flow into a machine intelligence could be increased by adding millions of sensors. Depending on the technology used, a machine might have reconfigurable hardware that can be optimized for changing task requirements, whereas much of the brain’s architecture is fixed from birth or only slowly changeable (though the details of synaptic connectivity can change over shorter timescales, like days).〔[註 31](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-31)〕

**繁體中文**

• 可靠性、壽命、感測器等。機器智慧還可能具有其他各種硬體優勢。例如，生物神經元不如電晶體可靠。〔[註 30](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-30)〕由於帶有雜訊的運算需要冗餘編碼，以多個元件編碼單一位元的資訊，數位大腦若採用可靠且高精度的運算元件，便可能獲得一些效率提升。大腦工作幾個小時後就會疲倦，經過幾十年的主觀時間後，便開始不可逆地衰退；微處理器不受這些限制。增加數百萬個感測器，就能提高流入機器智慧的資料量。依所採技術不同，機器可能擁有可重新配置的硬體，能因應不斷變化的任務需求進行最佳化；大腦的架構則大多從出生時就已固定，或只能緩慢改變，雖然突觸連接的細節，可以在數天等較短的時間尺度上改變。〔[註 31](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-31)〕

**English**

At present, the computational power of the biological brain still compares favorably with that of digital computers, though top-of-the-line supercomputers are attaining levels of performance that are within the range of plausible estimates of the brain’s processing power.〔[註 32](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-32)〕 But hardware is rapidly improving, and the ultimate limits of hardware performance are vastly higher than those of biological computing substrates.

**繁體中文**

目前，生物大腦的運算能力與數位電腦相比，仍然毫不遜色；不過，頂尖超級電腦的表現，正逐漸達到對大腦處理能力所作之合理估計的範圍。〔[註 32](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-32)〕但硬體正在迅速進步，而且硬體效能的最終極限，遠高於生物運算基質的極限。

**English**

Digital minds will also benefit from major advantages in software:

**繁體中文**

數位心智也將受益於軟體上的重大優勢：

**English**

• Editability. It is easier to experiment with parameter variations in software than in neural wetware. For example, with a whole brain emulation one could easily trial what happens if one adds more neurons in a particular cortical area or if one increases or decreases their excitability. Running such experiments in living biological brains would be far more difficult.

**繁體中文**

• 可編輯性。在軟體中試驗參數變化，比在神經濕體（neural wetware）中容易。例如，對全腦模擬而言，要試試在某個皮質區域增加神經元，或提高、降低其興奮性，會發生什麼情況，相當容易。在活體生物大腦中進行這類實驗，則困難得多。

---

**English**

• Duplicability. With software, one can quickly make arbitrarily many high-fidelity copies to fill the available hardware base. Biological brains, by contrast, can be reproduced only very slowly; and each new instance starts out in a helpless state, remembering nothing of what its parents learned in their lifetimes.

**繁體中文**

• 可複製性。只要使用軟體，就能迅速製作任意多個高保真複本，填滿可用的硬體資源。相較之下，生物大腦只能非常緩慢地繁衍；而且每一個新個體起初都處於無助的狀態，完全不記得父母一生所學到的東西。

---

**English**

• Goal coordination. Human collectives are replete with inefficiencies arising from the fact that it is nearly impossible to achieve complete uniformity of purpose among the members of a large group—at least until it becomes feasible to induce docility on a large scale by means of drugs or genetic selection. A “copy clan” (a group of identical or almost identical programs sharing a common goal) would avoid such coordination problems.

**繁體中文**

• 目標協調。人類集體中充斥著各種效率損失，因為要讓大型群體的成員在目的上完全一致，幾乎不可能——至少在能以藥物或遺傳選擇，大規模誘發順從性之前是如此。「複本族群」（copy clan，一群共享同一目標、彼此相同或幾乎相同的程式）便能避免這類協調問題。

**English**

• Memory sharing. Biological brains need extended periods of training and mentorship whereas digital minds could acquire new memories and skills by swapping data files. A population of a billion copies of an AI program could synchronize their databases periodically, so that all the instances of the program know everything that any instance learned during the previous hour. (Direct memory transfer requires standardized representational formats. Easy swapping of high-level cognitive content would therefore not be possible between just any pair of machine intelligences. In particular, it would not be possible among first-generation whole brain emulations.)

**繁體中文**

• 記憶共享。生物大腦需要長時間的訓練與指導，數位心智卻可以藉由交換資料檔，取得新的記憶與技能。由十億個 AI 程式複本構成的群體，可以定期同步資料庫，讓該程式的所有執行個體，都知道任何一個個體在上一小時內學到的一切。（直接傳輸記憶，需要標準化的表徵格式。因此，並非任意兩個機器智慧之間，都能輕鬆交換高層次認知內容。尤其是第一代全腦模擬體，無法做到這一點。）

**English**

• New modules, modalities, and algorithms. Visual perception seems to us easy and effortless, quite unlike solving textbook geometry problems—this despite the fact that it takes a massive amount of computation to reconstruct, from the two-dimensional patterns of stimulation on our retinas, a three-dimensional representation of a world populated with recognizable objects. The reason this seems easy is that we have dedicated low-level neural machinery for processing visual information. This low-level processing occurs unconsciously and automatically, without draining our mental energy or conscious attention. Music perception, language use, social cognition, and other forms of information processing that are “natural” for us humans seem to be likewise supported by dedicated neurocomputational modules. An artificial mind that had such specialized support for other cognitive domains that have become important in the contemporary world—such as engineering, computer programming, and business strategy—would have big advantages over minds like ours that have to rely on clunky general-purpose cognition to think about such things. New algorithms may also be developed to take advantage of the distinct affordances of digital hardware, such as its support for fast serial processing.

**繁體中文**

• 新模組、新模態與新演算法。視覺知覺對我們而言似乎輕而易舉、毫不費力，與解答教科書上的幾何題截然不同——儘管要從視網膜上二維的刺激模式，重建出一個充滿可辨識物件之世界的三維表徵，需要龐大的運算量。之所以覺得容易，是因為我們有專門處理視覺資訊的低層次神經機制。這種低層次處理在無意識中自動進行，不會耗費我們的心力或有意識的注意力。音樂知覺、語言使用、社會認知，以及其他對人類而言「自然」的資訊處理形式，似乎也同樣受到專門神經運算模組的支援。假如人工心智在當代世界日益重要的其他認知領域——例如工程、電腦程式設計與商業策略——也擁有這類專門支援，就會比我們這種必須依賴笨重的通用認知能力來思考這些事情的心智，具有巨大優勢。我們也可能開發出新演算法，利用數位硬體獨有的能力，例如對高速串行處理的支援。

**English**

The ultimately attainable advantages of machine intelligence, hardware and software combined, are enormous.〔[註 33](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-33)〕 But how rapidly could those potential advantages be realized? That is the question to which we now turn.

**繁體中文**

把硬體與軟體合在一起看，機器智慧最終可能取得的優勢極為龐大。〔[註 33](./03-第三章-註釋-英繁對照.md#note-33)〕但這些潛在優勢能以多快的速度實現？這就是我們接下來要探討的問題。
