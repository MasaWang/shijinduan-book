## 註 1

**English**

A subsistence-level income today is about $400 (Chen and Ravallion 2010). A million subsistence-level incomes is thus $400,000,000. The current world gross product is about $60,000,000,000,000 and in recent years has grown at an annual rate of about 4% (compound annual growth rate since 1950, based on Maddison [2010]). These figures yield the estimate mentioned in the text, which of course is only an order-of-magnitude approximation. If we look directly at population figures, we find that it currently takes the world population about one and a half weeks to grow by one million; but this underestimates the growth rate of the economy since per capita income is also increasing. By 5000 bc, following the Agricultural Revolution, the world population was growing at a rate of about 1 million per 200 years—a great acceleration since the rate of perhaps 1 million per million years in early humanoid prehistory—so a great deal of acceleration had already occurred by then. Still, it is impressive that an amount of economic growth that took 200 years seven thousand years ago takes just ninety minutes now, and that the world population growth that took two centuries then takes one and a half weeks now. See also Maddison (2005).

**繁體中文**

如今，維持最低生存水準的收入約為 400 美元（Chen and Ravallion 2010）。因此，一百萬份這種收入相當於 4 億美元。目前的全球總產出約為 60 兆美元，近年的年成長率約為 4%（根據 Maddison〔2010〕，這是自 1950 年起的複合年成長率）。由這些數字便可得到正文中的估計；當然，它只是數量級上的近似。如果直接看人口數字，會發現目前全球人口增加一百萬，大約需要一週半；但這會低估經濟成長速度，因為每人平均收入也在增加。到了農業革命之後的西元前 5000 年，全球人口約每兩百年增加一百萬；相較於早期人類祖先史前時代可能每一百萬年才增加一百萬人的速度，已大幅加快。因此，在那個時候就已發生了相當程度的加速。不過，七千年前需要兩百年的經濟增長，如今只需九十分鐘；當年需要兩個世紀的人口增長，如今只需一週半，仍令人驚嘆。另見 Maddison（2005）。

## 註 22

**English**

One vocal critic during this period was Hubert Dreyfus. Other prominent skeptics from this era include John Lucas, Roger Penrose, and John Searle. However, among these only Dreyfus was mainly concerned with refuting claims about what practical accomplishments we should expect from existing paradigms in AI (though he seems to have been open to the possibility that new paradigms could go further). Searle’s target was functionalist theories in the philosophy of mind, not the instrumental powers of AI systems. Lucas and Penrose denied that a classical computer could ever be programmed to do everything that a human mathematician can do, but they did not deny that any particular function could in principle be automated or that AIs might eventually become very instrumentally powerful. Cicero remarked that “there is nothing so absurd but some philosopher has said it” (Cicero 1923, 119); yet it is surprisingly hard to think of any significant thinker who has denied the possibility of machine superintelligence in the sense used in this book.

**繁體中文**

Hubert Dreyfus 是這段時期一位積極發聲的批評者。當時其他知名的懷疑者包括 John Lucas、Roger Penrose 與 John Searle。不過，其中只有 Dreyfus 主要致力於反駁：我們可以期待既有 AI 典範達成哪些實際成果——儘管他似乎不排斥新典範可能走得更遠。Searle 針對的是心靈哲學中的功能主義理論，而非 AI 系統達成目的的能力。Lucas 與 Penrose 否認古典電腦能被編寫成完成人類數學家所能做的一切，但並未否認任何特定功能原則上可以自動化，也未否認 AI 最終可能在實現目標的能力上變得極其強大。Cicero 曾說：「沒有什麼荒謬到不曾有哲學家說過。」（Cicero 1923, 119）然而，要找出一位重要思想家，曾否認本書所指的機器超級智慧之可能性，卻出乎意料地困難。

<a id="note-30"></a>

## 註 30

**English**

A program p is a description of string x if p, run on (some particular) universal Turing machine U, outputs x; we write this as U(p) = x. (The string x here represents a possible world.) The Kolmogorov complexity of x is then K(x) := minₚ {ℓ(p) : U(p) = x}, where ℓ(p) is the length of p in bits. The “Solomonoff” probability of x is then defined as M(x) := ∑ₚ:U(p)=x 2^(−ℓ(p)), where the sum is defined over all (“minimal,” i.e. not necessarily halting) programs p for which U outputs a string starting with x (Hutter 2005).

**繁體中文**

如果程式 p 在某一臺特定的通用圖靈機 U 上執行時，輸出字串 x，那麼 p 就是 x 的一種描述，記為 U(p) = x。（這裡的字串 x 代表一個可能世界。）於是，x 的柯爾莫哥洛夫複雜度為 K(x) := minₚ {ℓ(p) : U(p) = x}，其中 ℓ(p) 是 p 以位元計算的長度。x 的「所羅門諾夫」機率則定義為 M(x) := ∑ₚ:U(p)=x 2^(−ℓ(p))；求和範圍是所有使 U 輸出以 x 開頭之字串的「最小」程式 p，也就是不一定會停機的程式（Hutter 2005）。

## 註 31

**English**

Bayesian conditioning on evidence E gives

P_posterior(w) = P_prior(w|E) = P_prior(E|w) P_prior(w) / P_prior(w).

(The probability of a proposition [like E] is the sum of the probability of the possible worlds in which it is true.)

**繁體中文**

根據證據 E 進行貝氏條件化，可得：

P_posterior(w) = P_prior(w|E) = P_prior(E|w) P_prior(w) / P_prior(w)。

（一個命題，例如 E，其機率就是所有使它為真的可能世界的機率總和。）

> 譯校註（非原文）：此處忠實保留所提供 PDF 的公式。原書分母印為 P_prior(w)，與標準貝氏定理不符；標準分母應為 P_prior(E)。此為原書疑似誤植，並非沿用 EPUB 的破損公式。

<a id="note-84"></a>

## 註 84

**English**

There is a substantial literature documenting the unreliability of expert forecasts in many domains, and there is every reason to think that many of the findings in this body of research apply to the field of artificial intelligence too. In particular, forecasters tend to be overconfident in their predictions, believing themselves to be more accurate than they really are, and therefore assigning too little probability to the possibility that their most-favored hypothesis is wrong (Tetlock 2005). (Various other biases have also been documented; see, e.g., Gilovich et al. [2002].) However, uncertainty is an inescapable fact of the human condition, and many of our actions unavoidably rely on expectations about which long-term consequences are more or less plausible: in other words, on probabilistic predictions. Refusing to offer explicit probabilistic predictions would not make the epistemic problem go away; it would just hide it from view (Bostrom 2007). Instead, we should respond to evidence of overconfidence by broadening our confidence intervals (or “credible intervals”)—i.e. by smearing out our credence functions—and in general we must struggle as best we can with our biases, by considering different perspectives and aiming for intellectual honesty. In the longer run, we can also work to develop techniques, training methods, and institutions that can help us achieve better calibration. See also Armstrong and Sotala (2012).

**繁體中文**

已有大量文獻記錄許多領域中專家預測的不可靠性，我們完全有理由認為，其中許多發現也適用於人工智慧領域。尤其是，預測者往往對自己的預測過度自信，以為自己比實際上更準確，因此為最看好的假說可能出錯這件事，分配了太低的機率（Tetlock 2005）。（其他各種偏差也已有文獻記錄；例如，見 Gilovich et al.〔2002〕。）然而，不確定性是人類處境中無法逃避的事實；我們的許多行動，也不可避免地依賴對哪些長期後果較可能、哪些較不可能的期待——換句話說，依賴機率預測。拒絕提出明確的機率預測，並不會讓知識上的問題消失，只會把它藏起來（Bostrom 2007）。面對過度自信的證據，我們應當擴大信賴區間（或「可信區間」），也就是把自己的信念機率函數攤得更開。總體而言，我們必須盡可能與自身偏差奮戰，考量不同觀點，並以智識誠實為目標。長期而言，也可以致力於發展技術、訓練方法與制度，協助我們讓判斷與實際結果更加校準。另見 Armstrong and Sotala（2012）。
